  ## Usage

  Crisp `glinr` surface with a light sweep layered under the label. The sweep takes the surface text colour at 35 percent, so it is visible on light and dark scopes and on every stage backdrop. Override with `shimmerColor` and `shimmerDuration`.

  ## Examples

  ## Accessibility

  - Renders through `Button`: native `<button>`, 2px accent focus ring with offset, `disabled` and `aria-busy` states.
  - Accessible name comes from the label. Decorative effect layers are `aria-hidden` and `pointer-events-none`.

  ## Reduced Motion

  The sweep renders only at motion level `full`. It is removed under `prefers-reduced-motion` and `data-glin-motion="none"`, leaving a normal button.
