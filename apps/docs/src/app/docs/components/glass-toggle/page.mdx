  ## Accessibility

  - Renders with `role="switch"` and `aria-checked`.
  - Supports keyboard activation (Space) and a visible focus ring.
  - Native form participation through `name`, `value`, `required` and `form`.
  - `loading` sets `aria-busy` and makes the toggle inert.
  - 44px minimum hit area at every size; thumb moves toward the inline end in RTL.
  - Disabled state reduces opacity and blocks interaction.

  ## Reduced Motion

  When `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is active, or `data-glin-motion` is `none` or `subtle`, the thumb and fill change state instantly with no sliding transform.
